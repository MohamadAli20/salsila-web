## Research summary / working translation

Kawashima's commentary describes Usman Imam as born in 1943, the youngest among many siblings. His father Tabunawan was imam of the mosque in the village rendered マゴナナ (Magonana) in the Japanese text. The mosque is traditionally associated with Sayyidna's return from Mecca. The father is described as a sixth-generation descendant of Sayyidna. Usman's unnamed mother was from Bayang. The village spelling should not be silently normalized to Magonaya without checking.

The intermediate ancestors are not named in this passage. Preserve a missing-ancestry span from Hadji Said to Tabunawan, followed by the direct father–son link to Usman Imam. Do not transfer the sixth-generation statement to Usman.

## Separate Alicasan account

Mamadra's sibling connection to Usman Imam, his half-brother connection to Ginaronay, and the maternal-cousin relationship between Usman's mother and Samurong are attributed to Alicasan, November 9, 2026. They are not asserted as facts from this Kawashima passage.
