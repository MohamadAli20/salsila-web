# Tabonawan ancestry — Ameroden sa Bayang

Source: https://acmaly88.page.tl/AMERODEN-SA-BAYANG.htm
Credit: Ontay & Marogong.Net. Checked November 9, 2026.

Paternal route in this account: Kadi sa Binidayan → Paki → unnamed child of Paki → Tabonawan, Imam sa Binidayan.
Maternal route: Tuan sa Binidayan → Amai Makapondag; Amai Makapondag and Bagur → Babaida; Babaida and the unnamed child of Paki → Tabonawan.
Bagur is listed under Inai Manoyogun and Toron Zaman. The diagram records these parents in Bagur’s note rather than inventing intermediate identities.

Tabonawan here is not explicitly identified as Usman Imam’s father. Similar name and imam title are leads for a possible identity match, not proof. The Sophia record’s Tabunawan and this published-salsila Tabonawan remain separate nodes connected by a tentative identity line. The unspecified Kadi and Tuan entries are not silently merged with the existing Hadji Said or Tuan sa Magonaya records.
