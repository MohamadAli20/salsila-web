# Okir-inspired interface accents

The scrollwork in assets/okir-scroll.svg is an original simplified vector illustration inspired by flowing plant forms. It is not a reproduction of an identified traditional motif or a claim of artisan-authenticated okir.

Cultural context: National Museum of the Philippines, Architecture and Furniture: https://www.nationalmuseum.gov.ph/our-collections/ethnology/architecture-and-furniture/ . The museum describes carved okir on torogan components and panolong.

Decorations are placed in header whitespace, never inside the genealogy canvas. They do not receive pointer events or screen-reader focus. Text and controls retain solid backgrounds; touch targets, focus rings, night mode and forced-color settings are preserved. No animation is introduced.
