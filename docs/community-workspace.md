# Community genealogy: first local workflow

Implemented private drafts, people, generation layout, cited parent–child claims, local review-request markers, research notes, status labels, a chronological action log, JSON export/import, cycle rejection and safe source links. No accounts, backend, other-user verification or public publishing are represented as available.

Before shared launch, implement authenticated ownership and server-side authorization; explicit reviewer invitations; evidence-specific reviews with immutable authorship; moderator decisions separate from contributor claims; living-person privacy and appropriate legal basis; corrections/reporting/removal procedures; and rights-aware handling of user submissions. A source citation or community-supported badge does not grant rights to reproduce protected material.

The local action history is user-supplied data, not a tamper-proof audit log. A conflicting note marks a connection as disputed but does not alter its parentage. Editing/deletion and actual moderator decisions are future features. A two-parent claim is represented by two separately sourced parent–child connections; no spouse relationship is inferred.
